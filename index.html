ABOUT THE EVENT

More Than Just Volleyball

Play. Compete. Support.

The Fundraising Tournament is an indoor volleyball event hosted by the Invercargill Men's Volleyball Team.

Teams will come together for a day of competition, teamwork and community while helping support our fundraising efforts.

Whether you're on the court or supporting from the sidelines, everyone is welcome to be part of the event.
